### About

I'm a **Forward Deployed Engineer**, Tech Lead and Solutions Architect at **Globant's Physical AI
Studio**, where I lead the Edge AI and Platforms practices. For 8+ years I've taken production
systems end to end for global brands across aviation, smart venues, sports &amp; parks
entertainment, utilities and manufacturing, embedding directly with clients from discovery to
production.

- 🧠 **Agentic AI platforms** — LLM reasoning cores that fuse computer vision, BLE beacons and
  enterprise subsystems into autonomous decision-making for real-time operations
- 👁️ **Computer vision at the edge** — custom YOLO on NVIDIA DeepStream, `0.95+ F1` at
  `<500ms` end-to-end latency, running on industrial hardware in live environments
- 📡 **IoT at scale** — device fleets of `20,000+` units and millions of smart meters:
  zero-touch provisioning, OTA updates, fleet monitoring and cybersecurity
- 🤖 **Robotics &amp; embedded** — RobOps platforms and intelligent edge devices for
  human-robot interaction
- 🎓 **M.Sc. @ Unicamp** — reinforcement learning for IoT network cyber-defense using the
  military Kill Chain taxonomy *(paper under review at Elsevier Internet of Things)*
